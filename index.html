How to play

Cut the white cells into tiles of the shape(s) shown, in any orientation/reflection. Black cells are blocked. A green glow appears around the board when you're done.

  • Drag across cells to outline a whole tile, or tap an edge to cycle it: line, then ✕ ("no line here"), then empty.
  • Check tells you whether your marks are right so far. Hint shows the next logical step.

Report a problem or send a comment. Based on L-panel, invented by Naoki Inaba, as presented in Alex Bellos' Puzzle Ninja.
