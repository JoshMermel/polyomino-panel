# To do

Ideas that are wanted but not scheduled.

- **Daily puzzle.** One puzzle per day picked from across shapes, with the difficulty varying by weekday (as in multiverse star battle). Gives friends a reason to come back.
- **Decide which other shapes to keep.** 16 single shapes ship (plus eight pairs). The domino and the I tetromino and pentomino were cut (every line shape solves the same way; the I tromino stays as the one line shape). The analysis (rule mix per shape, keep-lists of 5 / 8 / 11 shapes) says the other forced-only shapes (X5, Z5, T5, V5, U5) add little too; wait for friends' feedback from real play. To cut: edit `shapes` in `tools/books/recipes.json`, delete the cut books from `data/`, run `node tools/build_books.mjs --all --promote`. Pools in `tools/library/` are kept, so a cut is reversible (re-add the shape and rebuild).
- **Fewer difficulty tiers?** Five tiers (Beginner, Medium, Hard, Expert, Extreme) may be too many; each section has one book per tier. Revisit after friends play.
- **More themed books.** Ideas: by board character (wide open vs many blocked cells, narrow corridors), by technique, and a daily puzzle from a reserved slice of the pools. Books are dealt out by `tools/books/collections.json`; a puzzle lives in at most one book.
- **More two-shape puzzles.** Eight pairs ship (see the README). Ideas: more pairs (e.g. P+Y, W+F, L5+N5 generated fine at 8x8), three or more shapes, and puzzles that *must* use each shape a given number of times. The domino pairs (domino + V tromino, domino + L) found no unique puzzle at 8x8, and V tromino + L has no 10x10 book (only 8 puzzles). Beginner puzzles are scarce for pairs; if friends find the pair books too hard, generate more easy ones (`node tools/hunt_all.mjs --want Beginner,Medium`) or adjust the per-book tier mixes in `tools/books/recipes.json`.
- **Rectangular boards, trim wasted black edges.** A surprisingly large number of boards have a whole row or column (sometimes two edges) of black squares. Make the player handle rectangular boards (the generator and most of the code already take separate rows and columns) and edit those puzzles to remove the empty border rows/columns. Things to sort out: the puzzle hash includes the grid, so saved progress for trimmed puzzles would restart; books group puzzles by board size, which should become the trimmed size (or area); the solution string and rating don't change (blocked border cells play no part).
- **Hints about "every way to cover a cell" should skip cells that are already covered.** The edges, lookahead and chain hints reason about all placements of a tile over a cell; they shouldn't fire for a cell that is already inside a finished tile (a closed region of exactly one valid tile), since there is nothing left to deduce there.
- **Distinct color per shape in multi-shape puzzles.** Give each allowed shape its own color in the header pictures and use that color to fill a tile once the player closes a valid one (the region analysis already knows which shape a closed region is). Needs a small palette that works in light and dark mode and doesn't clash with the existing blue / red / purple / green / yellow highlights.
- **Easier erasing, for solving by short guess chains (to analyze).** Erasing is awkward today and a natural way to solve is to guess a tile and follow it. Ideas: re-dragging over a shape removes only the edges the player added that belong to that shape alone (not edges shared with a neighbouring tile); a temporary "guess layer" in its own color that can be accepted or discarded in one tap; double-tap a tile to clear its interior; or undo to a named checkpoint. Compare against how star battle handles guesses.
- **Shape filter in Browse.** Books mix shapes; let the player filter the Browse grid (and "First unsolved") by shape or pair, so someone who likes one shape can play just those puzzles.
- **Phone edge-tap target.** The edge hit area is about 11 px wide on a 10x10 board on a phone (0.34 of a cell), so a tap slightly off an edge does nothing. Needs a decision (wider tolerance for touch vs room to start a tile trace) and a real-device test; when both an h and a v edge match near a corner, pick the nearer one.
- **Real-device checks.** Offline play (service worker) and the print dialog can't be exercised in the built-in browser pane; try them in a real browser/phone. The iOS Simulator needs a full Xcode install.
- **Big pools make rescoring workers die.** `tools/rescore_library.mjs` workers were killed (probably out of memory) on the largest 10x10 pools; the per-puzzle placement caches stay alive for the whole file.
