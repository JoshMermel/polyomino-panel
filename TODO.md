## Ideas for more experiments

- **Cutouts follow-ups.** `bonus-shapes` (shown as Bonus: cutouts) has 400 puzzles on 16 board shapes with 20 tile specs (12 single tiles, 8 pairs). Ideas: more outlines (a heart, a cross with arms of unequal length, a hexagon), sparser puzzles (extra blocked cells start at about 6% of the open cells), three-shape puzzles, a pool-based pipeline like the main books.
- **Hex and triangle follow-ups.** Pairs of tiles; other outlines (triangle boards: trapezoids, stars; hexes: a honeycomb with a hole); hexagon boards of side 7; a parity-like rule for the 3-colouring of hexes. Triangle tilings are rigid, so the pools have few Hard and Expert puzzles (Medium triangles have 5 Expert): pairs of tiles, sparser boards or the hunter would find more, but the books are deliberately gentle now. The pools the books are picked from (about 24,000 puzzles per grid) are not in the repo; the commands to make them are in README.md (`make_shaped.mjs` per board and tile, then `make_grid_book.mjs`).
- **Intro variety.** The Intro now has 20 extra-small hex puzzles (the 5x5 section); extra-small triangles (hexagon of side 1 or 2, triangles of 4 and 5) could join them the same way (`make_grid_book.mjs` has a `hexintro` mode to copy; the Intro group's `extra` takes any number of source books).
- **Progress when books change.** Regenerating a source book (hexes, triangles, cutouts, giants) renames its puzzles by position, so a player's solved marks on it no longer line up. A rebuild that keeps puzzles in place (as the main books do) would avoid that if these books are ever regenerated again.
- **Printing follow-ups.** A paper-size choice (A4 or Letter: the layout assumes the smaller of both), grouping a mixed-size selection (Intro, cutouts) by board size so a small board does not take a big board's grid, a printed answer key at a smaller cell size on 16x16 and bigger boards.
