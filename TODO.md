## Open, with a recommendation

- **Ghost the failing tile in lookahead hints.** Show the tile being tried (ghosted) with the cell or enclosed region it breaks, so the explanation is visual and not only text. It may be too busy next to the yellow cell, the "!" marker and the dashed lines; try it on the second press of the step-by-step hints. *Recommendation: try it; it is a small change and the hints are the most-used help.*
- **Enclosed-region and parity try-a-line rules.** Measured 2026-11-08 on 2,267 Hard puzzles: an enclosed-region cross as a Medium rule re-bins 2% of Hard puzzles to Medium and rescues none of the Expert ones; the parity version applies in 3 of 2,267. *Recommendation: drop it.*
- **More ways to erase.** Tap a closed tile twice (or long-press) to erase it; a rubber-band drag that clears every line inside a region (today a drag over cells that are not a tile only shows a toast). *Recommendation: skip unless people ask; Undo and Reset cover it.*
- **More varied Beginner puzzles on sparse boards.** Only the line pieces and a few rigid shapes make Beginner puzzles under 17% blocked, so Beginner is 15 to 40 puzzles per book (13 in `three-6`, none in `three-8` and `three-10`). Ideas: a per-tier `maxBlocked` for Beginner (say 0.30), hunting sparse Beginner puzzles (`tools/hunt_library.mjs --want Beginner`), or treating the gentlest Medium puzzles as the opening. *Recommendation: a per-tier `maxBlocked` for Beginner (one config line), if the Beginner tier feels thin.*
- **More deduction rules (the Expert tier).** Expert is now its own tier (the puzzles the rules cannot finish): 50 in every main book except `one-6`. Two stronger rules solved 52 of 53 of the stuck puzzles in an earlier experiment: a chain whose hypothesis is propagated with forced tiles AND edge deductions (38 of them), and "eliminate, then edges". *Recommendation: skip; Expert as a tier means players know what they are getting, and every rule added re-rates every book.*
- **Diversity guard in the book builder.** Make `tools/build_books.mjs` skip a puzzle too close (blocked cells differing in under about 8% of the cells) to one already in the book, so books stay varied when a pool has siblings. *Recommendation: skip unless a book visibly repeats itself; `prune_library.mjs` already thins the pools.*
